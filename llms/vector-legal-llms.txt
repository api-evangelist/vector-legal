# Vector Legal

> Vector Legal is an AI-native law firm for startups. Experienced startup attorneys handle formation, financing, commercial contracts, and ongoing counsel, supported by proprietary software that speeds up preparation without replacing legal judgment. Legal services are provided by Vector Legal, P.C., a licensed California law firm; the platform is built by Vector Systems, PBC.

Vector Legal serves startups from formation through Series B and beyond. Work is priced around the outcome, not billable hours: most matters are a fixed fee agreed up front, fast-moving teams can retain the firm monthly, and complex transactions are scoped to the objective, so clients know the cost and scope before work begins. Vector Legal is backed by Y Combinator, Base10, Palm Drive Capital, and Amino Capital. Headquartered in San Francisco, California. Contact: contact@vectorlegal.com.
